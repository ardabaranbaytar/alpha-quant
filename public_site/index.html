THE SCOPE OF THIS RECORD

Completed simulations.
Clearly defined results.

Alpha Quant is an experimental pairs-trading research project. This journal is designed to report completed simulated trades and their realized outcomes.

Historical price simulation.

The data source and simulation assumptions accompany each generated results snapshot.

01

What is included

Only completed simulated trades with a verified closing timestamp and a numeric net profit or loss. The record includes both profitable and losing trades.

02

What remains private

Open positions, current trade signals, entry thresholds, and active exposure are excluded from the public record.

03

How results are measured

Net P&L is the sum of completed trade outcomes after modeled transaction costs. Win rate is the share of completed trades with a positive result. Profit factor is total positive P&L divided by the absolute total of negative P&L; it is undefined when there are no losses.

04

Reading the chart

The curve begins at zero and accumulates realized P&L in closing order. Maximum realized drawdown is the largest drop from a previous peak, including the starting zero balance. This is not a mark-to-market equity curve and does not measure risk from open positions.

05

Periods and timestamps

Reporting periods use closing dates and end at the snapshot timestamp, not the current date. Each selected period starts its own cumulative P&L at zero. All dates are displayed in UTC. Holding time is elapsed calendar time between opening and closing.

06

Simulation assumptions

Simulated outcomes are not real investment returns. Actual execution quality and liquidity may differ.

07

Limits of the model

Daily prices are adjusted for splits and dividends. P&L uses synthetic adjusted shares, not broker fills or a full corporate-action ledger. Short availability, changing borrow rates, liquidity constraints, and margin calls are not modeled. Open positions are excluded and are not forcibly closed at the end of the test, so closed-only P&L is not total portfolio performance.
